# Poseidon x FitVoyages — Phrase Bank

Grouped by moment. Reach for what fits, don't force all of them in.

---

### Positioning Poseidon

- "You're closer to the vessel; we're closer to the regulation."
- "What you're solving is what happened on the vessel. What we solve is what's owed, and whether the answer survives an audit."
- "We're narrow on purpose — one regulatory problem, done properly."
- "We don't touch sensors or hardware — that's not our layer."
- "Your validated data is exactly the single source of truth we'd want to consume, not rebuild."

---

### Staying Honest Under Scrutiny

- "That's a fair question and I don't want to guess at it — I'd rather check and follow up than give you a confident answer that's wrong."
- "Possible — walk me through how you're reading it, I'd rather get this right than defend a position."
- "Not yet in v1 — that's exactly the kind of thing your integration would fix."
- "I'd rather be precise than impressive."
- "We prioritised getting the regulatory mechanics right before scaling — it's a penalty-bearing product."

---

### Showing Domain Depth Without Lecturing

- "EU ETS is an allowance obligation — fuel weighted by coverage and phase-in, against a surrender deadline. Happy to go deeper or keep it high-level."
- "FuelEU isn't a payment for emissions — it's a limit on intensity, measured well-to-wake, against a target that tightens every period."
- "The audit trail is really the part that matters most under scrutiny."
- "Regulation moves — the discipline is keeping up with it, not just building it once."

---

### Listening Mode (understanding FitVoyages)

- "Where does your validated data stop and a client's own reporting begin today?"
- "Can you attribute fuel consumption to a specific voyage, and how far back does the historical data go?"
- "Do you capture shore-power consumption, or do any clients blend biofuels?"
- "Have any of your current clients asked you directly about EU ETS or FuelEU?"

---

### Mapping the Overlap

- "That sounds like exactly the kind of validated input we'd want to consume rather than rebuild."
- "If that split holds up on your side too, the interesting question is what a clean handoff looks like."
- "We'd want to be a consumer of your data, not a competitor to it."
- "The highest-value handoff is vessel identity, voyages, and fuel by type and quantity — we already have the machinery to compute the rest."

---

### If "Are You Competing With Us" Comes Up

- "No — different buyer, Mediterranean yachts versus OSV. Any move into your segment would be through partnership."
- "If anything, we'd want to make your data more valuable to a client, not compete with it."
- "I wouldn't want to go near your segment without going through a partnership with you first."

---

### If the Room Isn't Who You Expected

- "Good to meet you all — before I get into Poseidon, I'd love to understand how each of you fits into what FitVoyages is building."
- "There's a few of you, so I want to make sure I'm pitched at the right level of detail — happy to go deeper technically wherever it's useful."

---

### Greece / The Pilot

- "We're anchored in the Mediterranean — if your OSV clients are trading into EU ports, the same deadlines apply to them."
- "The cleanest way to test this is one vessel, one workflow — if you have a client making EU calls, we map its fuel and voyage data into a single EU ETS position."
- "Realistically the first shape is a data-feed handoff plus a referral — low-commitment, proves the mechanics."

---

### Closing & Next Steps

- "I'd like to leave with one concrete next step, even a small one."
- "Would it make sense to have a narrower, technical follow-up between our engineering and yours on data format?"
- "I'll send a short note with a proposed scope by Friday."
- "Let's put a date on the next conversation rather than leave it open-ended."

---

### General Credibility Markers

- "I'd rather be precise than impressive."
- "We're building for defensibility first, not speed."
- "That's a fair challenge — let me answer it directly."
