Regulatory Clock

Time remaining until EUA surrender

The EU ETS EUA surrender deadline is 30 September 2026, covering the 2025 reporting year at its 70% phase-in rate. 2026 is the first year of full (100%) coverage. Operators must surrender EUAs equal to their verified emissions — non-surrender triggers an excess-emissions penalty (€100 per tonne, index-adjusted) plus the outstanding obligation. Separately, FuelEU Maritime's first penalty cycle is already active since June 2026 (~€2,400 per tonne VLSFO-equivalent) for GHG-intensity shortfalls.

How It Works

Compliance infrastructure, not a dashboard

Poseidon Ledger ingests the operational documents you already produce — bunker delivery notes, noon reports and charter parties — and turns them into a regulator-ready compliance record with calculations you can reproduce and defend.

Document Ingestion & OCR

BDNs, noon reports and charter parties become structured, queryable records via OCR with quality scoring and a human review queue. Fuel type, quantity, sulphur content and ports are extracted — not re-typed into a spreadsheet.

BDN · noon · charter party · quality review workflow

02
⬡

Med SOx ECA Watch

MARPOL Annex VI Reg. 14: the 0.10% sulphur limit applies inside the Mediterranean SOx ECA (effective 1 May 2025). Designated rules — SOX-ECA-01..06 — evaluate zone entry/exit events and BDN sulphur evidence against the applicable limit, with alerts. Deterministic, not AI-guessed.

Active since 1 May 2025 · zone events · BDN sulphur evidence

03
◎

EU ETS Engine

Each voyage is classified for coverage (intra-EU 100%, EU ↔ third country 50%, non-EU 0%). The engine applies the phase-in schedule (40% → 70% → 100%) to compute the EUA obligation and estimated cost against the 30 September surrender deadline.

Versioned parameters · 2026 = 100% phase-in · surrender clock

04
⊞

FuelEU Maritime

Well-to-wake GHG intensity (gCO₂e/MJ) is computed from fuel energy inputs and compared against the 2025–29 target (91.16 baseline, 2% reduction). Compliance balance, banking and pooling, ISCC biofuel flags and a penalty exposure estimate (€2,400 / tonne VLSFOe).

Baseline 91.16 gCO₂e/MJ · balance & pooling · penalty estimate

05
◇

EU MRV & Verifier Package

Completeness checks and a pre-submission checklist, a THETIS-MRV XML export matching the EMSA upload schema (manual web-portal upload), and a verifier package builder + validator with a content hash so a report can't be altered silently.

THETIS-MRV XML · verifier package · content hash

⊕

Audit Trail & Review

Every number traces back to its inputs and method. If there's a gap or a quality question, it goes to a review queue rather than being silently estimated — the difference between a number and a defensible number.
